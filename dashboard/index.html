How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × chain multiplier × crowding × growth multiplier × bleed × maturity multiplier × completeness. The category multiple is the live market's own price: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live protocols in that category, shrunk in log space toward the global median, so one outlier cannot drag a whole category. Chains are weighed, not counted: each chain's weight is its share of the live set's TVL, and the multiplier is 1 + 0.70 × the summed weight of the protocol's chains — Ethereum alone pays 1.28, five chains nobody's TVL sits on pay 1.02. Crowding discounts a launch by 1 / (1 + 0.06 × ln(n)) where n is the count of pre-token protocols in its category. A seven-day bleed below −20% costs up to a further 15%. Maturity grows with ln(1 + age/365); a missing seven-day change costs 3% and an unreported listing date a further 2%. No protocol name is ever special-cased — the same function scores every row, and the farm plan farms on the protocol's cheapest chain.

Implied value is a model, not a price, and a launch is never guaranteed. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry.
